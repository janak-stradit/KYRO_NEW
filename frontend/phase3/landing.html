How KYRO works

Continuous reviews, clear justifications

KYRO performs periodic reviews by monitoring investor transactions and risk scores (high, medium, low) using rules and AI to detect anomalies, reduce false positives, and provide clear review justifications.

  • Scores every customer and transaction as Low, Medium or High risk
  • Flags deviations from each customer's behavioral baseline
  • Routes high-confidence alerts to analysts for review
  • Records why every alert fired, for auditors and regulators
